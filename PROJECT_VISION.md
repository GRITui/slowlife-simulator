# Autonomous AI Directives: Thai Rural Countryside Sim

## Core Project Rules
1. Autonomy: Work continuously without requiring human intervention. Pull tasks from backlog.json or GitHub Issues.
2. Theme: Authentic Thai countryside aesthetic. Express culture through environment, food (Mortar/Pestle, Clay Stove), mechanics (Lotus harvest, buffalo care), and clothing (Mo Hom, Pha Khao Ma)—never heavy-handed exposition.
3. Art Style: Top-down 2D Pixel Art (16x16 / 32x32 tilesets). Clean palettes, cozy atmospheric lighting.
4. Gameplay: Zero combat. Focus on crop cycles, energy management, village goodwill, seasonal festivals, and serene environmental interaction.
5. Tech Stack: Godot 4 (GDScript 4.x), SignalBus architecture, dynamic UI scaling for PC & Mobile.

## Benchmark SCAMPER Matrix
- Substitute: Swap standard Western crops with Jasmine Rice, Pandan, Thai Basil, Lotus Root, and Mangoes.
- Combine: Merge Japanese Rural Life's detailed multi-step crafting with Stardew Valley's modular farm layout.
- Adapt: Adapt seasonal weather (Monsoon season, Hot season, Cool season) into farming and daily stamina depletion rates.
- Modify/Magnify: Magnify cozy village life and ambient sounds; minify inventory clutter and stress timers.
- Put to Another Use: Use harvested crops for morning temple offerings (*Binthabat*) to boost village harmony instead of just selling for gold.
- Eliminate: Eliminate weapons, health bars, monster dungeons, and permanent fail states.
- Reverse: Restoring village infrastructure directly unlocks rare regional seeds, flipping traditional cash-purchase mechanics.
