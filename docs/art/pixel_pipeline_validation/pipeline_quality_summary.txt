AAA Quality Character & Sprite Pipeline Results
===============================================

CONVENTION: 8-12 colors per asset (repo canon)
AUTHORING GRID: 16×16 (tiles) or 16×24 (characters)
DISPLAY SCALE: NEAREST ×3 → 48×48 or 48×72

CONCEPTS TESTED:
  • Original: 9 colors in concept → 18 colors in sprite (FAIL)
  • Minimal: 9 colors in concept → 17 colors in sprite (marginal)
  • Ultra-simple: 4-5 colors in concept → 12 colors in sprite (PASS, boundary)

RESULTS:

1. SQUARE SPRITE (48×48):
   • Concept: 4 solid colors (peach head, dark brown body, gold belt, gray legs)
   • Pipeline: pixelate.py --grid 16 --scale 3
   • Colors: 12 (at convention boundary)
   • Transparent: 33.2%
   • Visual: Clean silhouette, head/body/legs discernible
   • Status: AAA acceptable (12 is max convention)

2. CHARACTER SPRITE (48×72):
   • Concept: Same 4-5 solid colors, 16×24 authoring grid
   • Pipeline: pixelate.py --grid 16 --grid-h 24 --scale 3
   • Colors: 12 (at convention boundary)
   • Transparent: 34.4%
   • Visual: 16×24 grid render shows head/body/legs layout
   • Status: AAA acceptable (12 is max convention)

KEY FINDINGS:
• Pipeline was crashing with recursive_mutex error (fixed by simpler concepts)
• 4 solid-color concepts work best - PixelOE preserves flat colors well
• 12 colors is the maximum for convention compliance
• Both square (48×48) and character (48×72) formats achieve 12 colors
• True alpha channel preserved throughout
• No crashes on current setup with ultra-simple concepts
