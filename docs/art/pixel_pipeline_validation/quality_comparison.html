Key findings:

  • Pipeline crashed on complex concepts (recursive_mutex error)
  • 4 solid-color concepts work best — PixelOE preserves flat colors
  • 12 colors is the maximum for 8–12 convention compliance
  • Both square (48×48) and character (48×72) formats achieve 12 colors
  • True alpha channel preserved throughout

Pipeline command:

PY=~/.venvs/artpipe/bin/python
$PY tools/pixel_pipeline/pixelate.py /tmp/ultra_simple.png /tmp/output.png \
  --grid 16 --scale 3 --center-frac 0.9

or for characters:

$PY tools/pixel_pipeline/pixelate.py /tmp/ultra_simple.png /tmp/output.png \
  --grid 16 --grid-h 24 --scale 3 --center-frac 0.9
